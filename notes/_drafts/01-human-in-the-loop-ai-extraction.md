# Draft outline: Human-in-the-loop AI extraction

**Working title:** "We didn't replace the reviewers. We stopped making them type."
**Shows:** Outcomes over output · Optimization machine · responsible AI in a regulated industry
**Length:** 800–1,200 words, in your own voice
**Sensitivity:** describe what was built, not TEAM's systems, vendors, document types or exact numbers. Give your manager a heads-up before posting.

1. **The hook.** High-volume documents keyed in by hand: slow, error-prone, and every mistake shows up later as reconciliation work.
2. **Why "just use an LLM" isn't the answer** in a regulated environment: accuracy, auditability, trust.
3. **What we built.** The extraction application, the human-in-the-loop review step ("approve, don't transcribe"), and the integration that writes clean data downstream.
4. **The decision that mattered most.** Pick one real trade-off you made (where to put the human, what confidence threshold, what *not* to automate).
5. **Reuse.** Extending the same pipeline to a second workflow, and why building it as a pipeline made that cheap.
6. **Result, kept general.** "Millions a year, manual entry and reconciliation nearly gone."
7. **Takeaway for other PMs.** One sentence they can steal.
