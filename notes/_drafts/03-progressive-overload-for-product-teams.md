# Draft outline: Progressive overload for product teams

**Working title:** "Progressive overload for product teams"
**Shows:** Grit and determination · Optimization machine · the fitness side of you, kept light
**Length:** 700–1,000 words, in your own voice. This one can be more personal.

1. **The gym idea.** Progressive overload: small, consistent increases beat occasional heroic sessions.
2. **The product translation.** Sprints as training blocks: steady velocity and measured progress over crunch-and-crash.
3. **Deload weeks.** Why teams (and lifters) need planned recovery, and what that looks like in a roadmap.
4. **Track the lift.** You log sets and reps; teams should log the few metrics that matter, not everything.
5. **A real example.** The Agile rollout that raised delivery velocity by 300% (Procare): what the "small consistent increases" actually were.
6. **Close.** One line connecting the two.
