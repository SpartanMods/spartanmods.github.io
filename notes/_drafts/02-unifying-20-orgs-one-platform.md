# Draft outline: Unifying 20 organizations onto one data platform

**Working title:** "20 organizations, one platform: what I said no to"
**Shows:** Grit and determination · Team captain · strategic thinking (the biggest gap on the site today)
**Length:** 800–1,200 words, in your own voice

1. **The starting point.** 20 organizations, 120+ source systems, no single version of the truth.
2. **The strategy call.** What you prioritized first and why. This is the most important section: name at least one thing you deliberately did *not* build.
3. **Getting executives aligned.** How you got leaders across organizations to agree on one roadmap.
4. **Shipping 0 to 1.** How the team (and Agile adoption) got it from 0 to 1.
5. **What came next.** Payroll, operations and clinical data on the same platform. One line, kept general.
6. **What you'd do differently.** Honesty here builds more trust than another win.
